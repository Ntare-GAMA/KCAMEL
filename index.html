Who We Are

One Word.
Cinematographer.

KCAMEL is a premier film and music production house based in Kigali, Rwanda. We don't just capture moments — we architect them. From mastering light on a Kigali rooftop to scripting the emotional arc of an entire production, every frame carries deliberate intention.

Managing Director NTARE GAMA Allan is the full creative stack in one person — director, gaffer, scriptwriter. Cinematic vision meets technical mastery, delivering stories that resonate across Rwanda and beyond.
